#### Weeding

A weed is competing with your plants for the same water, light, and root space.
Pulling it early is the whole job.

Weed after rain or a watering, when the ground is soft and roots come up whole.
Get a hand under the crown and pull steadily rather than snatching, so the root
comes with the top. Perennial weeds regrow from any root left behind, and a
snapped dandelion root makes two dandelions.

The timing that matters most is before seed. A weed pulled in flower costs you
five minutes. The same weed left three weeks drops seed that you will be pulling
for several years.

Mulch does most of this work for you. A 2–3in layer keeps weed seeds in the dark,
and what does come through pulls easily out of loose mulch rather than packed
soil.

Do not clear the ground bare and leave it. Open soil is an invitation, and
something will take it. Plant it, or cover it.

##### Tools
- Gloves
- Hand fork or weeding knife
- Hoe, for seedlings in open ground
- Kneeler or pad
