#### Improving the soil

Soil is improved by adding organic matter and then leaving it alone. Both halves
matter.

Spread an inch of compost over the surface each spring and let the worms take it
down. Over several seasons this opens up clay so it drains, and gives sand
something to hold water with. It is slow and it is the only method that keeps
working.

Do not dig it in. Turning soil over breaks the structure that gives it its
drainage and air, kills the fungal networks the plants are feeding through, and
brings a fresh set of weed seeds up to the light. Ground that is dug every year
gets worse, not better.

Stay off wet ground. Walking on soggy soil compacts it, and compaction on clay
takes years to undo. If your boot leaves a print, wait.

Beds cannot be fixed in a season, but they do not need to be. A bed given
compost every spring is noticeably better in its third year, and the plants show
it before the soil does.

##### Tools
- Wheelbarrow and shovel
- Garden fork, for loosening without turning
- Compost
