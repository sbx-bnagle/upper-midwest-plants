#### Pinch

Pinching is taking out the growing tip of a stem between finger and thumb, early
in the season, before flower buds have formed.

Do it in late spring or early summer while the stems are soft enough to snap.
Removing the tip forces the two buds below it to break, so one stem becomes two
and the plant ends shorter and wider. On tall perennials that flop in a summer
storm, this is the fix that works.

Pinch after buds have set and you remove the flowers with them, delaying bloom
by weeks or losing it. Pinch nothing and a tall plant may still stand, but on an
open site it will need staking instead.

Use your fingers. Stems soft enough to pinch are soft enough that tools add
nothing.
