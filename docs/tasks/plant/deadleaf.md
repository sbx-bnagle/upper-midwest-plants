#### Deadleaf

Deadleafing is pulling or cutting away individual leaves that have browned,
torn, or spotted, while the rest of the plant carries on.

Take the whole leaf, stalk included, back to where it joins the crown. A leaf
snapped off halfway leaves a stub that browns in turn. Do it whenever you notice
damage rather than on a schedule.

Leaves spotted with disease carry the spores that spread it, so those go in the
bin and not the compost. Left in place, they reinfect the new growth beneath
them through the season.

Nothing here is delicate work. A plant can lose a third of its leaves and not
notice.

##### Tools
- Snips or hand pruners
- Gloves
- A bag for diseased material
