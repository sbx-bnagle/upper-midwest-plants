#### Shear

Shearing is cutting the whole clump back at once to an even height, rather than
stem by stem.

Do it after the first flush of bloom has passed, taking off the top third to
half. Work around the plant keeping the blade level, and do not try to follow
the shape of individual stems. New growth comes from below the cut within two
weeks and the plant closes back over.

Shear too late in the season and the regrowth will not harden before frost.
Shear too hard, below the lowest leaves, and some plants will not break again.

Judging the height by eye is close enough. An uneven cut disappears as the plant
fills in.

##### Tools
- Hedge shears
- Hand pruners for tidying afterwards
