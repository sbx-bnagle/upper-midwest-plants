#### Water deeply

Watering deeply means giving enough at one time to wet the soil right through
the root zone, then not watering again until it has dried.

Aim to wet the top 6–9in. Check by digging a small hole after watering, or push
a spade in and lever the soil open. Water in the early morning so the leaves
dry, and put the water on the ground rather than over the plant.

Little and often is the mistake worth avoiding. It keeps the top inch damp, and
roots grow where the water is, so they stay near the surface where they cook in
July and dry out in a week without rain. Deep watering sends them down, and a
deeply rooted plant will carry itself through a dry spell you would otherwise
have to manage.

An established plant tolerates going dry between waterings. That is the point of
the method.

##### Tools
- Soaker hose or drip line, or a hose left running slowly
- Spade, for checking the depth
