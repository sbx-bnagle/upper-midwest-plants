### Early spring

../../tasks/plant/thin.md

../../tasks/plant/prune.md

**Spiraea:** Most garden spireas (bumald and birchleaf types, e.g. Anthony Waterer, Goldflame, Goldmound) bloom on the current year's growth and are pruned back in late winter/early spring. A few old-fashioned spring bloomers (Vanhoutte, Grefsheim) bloom on old wood instead and should be pruned right after flowering, not in spring.
