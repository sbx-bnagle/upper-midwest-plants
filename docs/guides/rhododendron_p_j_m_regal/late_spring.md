### Late spring

../../tasks/plant/deadhead.md

**Rhododendron:** Snap or prune off faded flower clusters just below the flower head shortly after bloom, taking care not to damage the new leaf buds at the base of the flower stalk — this redirects energy from seed production into next year's buds.

../../tasks/plant/prune.md

**Rhododendron:** The window right after flowering is the best time for any shaping or size-control pruning; heavy pruning later risks removing next year's flower buds.
