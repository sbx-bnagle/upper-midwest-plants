### Mid summer

../../tasks/plant/shear.md

**Phlox divaricata:** Using hedge shears, cut off any browning flowering stems down to the green foliage. In hot, humid conditions Phlox divaricata may develop powdery mildew — shear down to promote lush, new growth.

../../tasks/plant/monitor.md

**Phlox divaricata:** If allowed to dry out in the summer, or if given too much sun, the foliage may die back.
