../../tasks/plant/space.md

**Phlox:** Give generous spacing and airflow; crowding drives powdery mildew.

../../tasks/plant/monitor.md

**Phlox:** Powdery mildew in hot, humid weather, especially under stress from heat or drought. Resistance varies sharply by cultivar — Mt. Cuba's 3-year trial of 94 selections found the top performers (led by 'Jeana') were almost all U.S.-origin selections, not the European cultivars often marketed as resistant.

../../tasks/plant/plant_timing.md

**Phlox divaricata:** Plant in spring to avoid winter frost heaving.
