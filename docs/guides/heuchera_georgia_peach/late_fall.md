**Heuchera:** Newly planted Heuchera are especially prone to frost heave in their first winter, as freeze-thaw cycles lift the shallow-rooted crown out of the soil; mulch heavily after the first hard frost to buffer temperature swings and add weight. Plants are less at risk once the root system is established after a couple of years.
