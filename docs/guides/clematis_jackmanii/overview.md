**Clematis:** Identify the pruning group before cutting. Group 1: flowers only on old wood — prune after spring flowering. Group 2: flowers on old and new wood — little pruning needed; cutting to the ground or pruning in fall/spring reduces or delays bloom but doesn't prevent it. Group 3: flowers only on new wood — can be cut to the ground in fall or early spring before growth emerges.
