### Mid summer

../../tasks/plant/divide.md

**Iris:** Divide every 3-5 years once flowering declines or clumps get crowded; division also helps manage iris borer and soft rot. Best done in July-August, after bloom.

../../tasks/plant/monitor.md

**Iris:** When dividing, discard any rhizome that is soft, mushy, or tunneled by iris borer rather than replanting it.

../../tasks/plant/cutback.md

**Iris:** Cut foliage back to about a third of its height at division time to reduce water loss and transplant stress.
