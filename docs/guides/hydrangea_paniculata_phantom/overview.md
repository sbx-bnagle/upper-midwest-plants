**Hydrangea:** H. arborescens (smooth hydrangea): once established, prune in spring down to the ground; blooms on new wood. H. paniculata (panicle hydrangea): prune back to a fat bud in fall, late winter, or spring. H. macrophylla (bigleaf hydrangea): buds form in late summer/early fall for next year, so prune right after flowering and before August, or you remove next year's blooms.
