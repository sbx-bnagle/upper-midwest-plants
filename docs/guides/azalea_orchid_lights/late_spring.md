### Late spring

../../tasks/plant/deadhead.md

**Azalea:** Snap or prune off faded flower clusters just after bloom, taking care not to damage the new leaf buds at the base of the flower stalk.

../../tasks/plant/prune.md

**Azalea:** Any shaping or size-control pruning belongs in this same right-after-bloom window — waiting until later removes the buds that would have bloomed next spring.
